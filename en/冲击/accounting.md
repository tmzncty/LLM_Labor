# Accounting

> Accounting is considered the "iron rice bowl" among "iron rice bowls"—every company needs accountants, and the more complex tax laws become, the more professionals are needed. But AI is rewriting this logic. Basic bookkeeping, report generation, invoice processing—these tasks, which account for over 70% of accounting workload, are being rapidly automated by AI tools. The Big Four accounting firms are already deploying AI on a large scale. Junior accountant positions are shrinking, and those who remain发现自己从"professionals"变成了"AI reviewers."

## I. Before the Impact: What This Profession Used to Be

Accounting is one of the world's oldest professions. From ancient Mesopotamian clay tablet ledgers to modern ERP systems, accounting has always been the infrastructure of business operations.

**Categories of Accounting**:

- **Financial Accounting**: Preparing financial statements and reporting corporate financial status to external stakeholders
- **Management Accounting**: Providing financial analysis and recommendations for internal management decisions
- **Tax Accounting**: Handling tax filings, tax planning, and tax compliance
- **Auditing**: Reviewing the authenticity and compliance of financial statements
- **Bookkeeping**: Daily accounting processing—recording transactions, reconciling accounts, closing books

**Market Size**:
- In 2023, the global accounting services market was valued at approximately $600 billion[^1]
- The U.S. accounting industry is approximately $140 billion[^2]
- China's accounting services market is approximately 300 billion RMB[^3]

**Number of Practitioners**:
- United States: approximately 1.4 million accountants and auditors[^4]
- China: approximately 20 million accounting practitioners[^5]
- Global: approximately 50 million accounting practitioners[^6]

**Income Levels**:
- United States: Junior accountants approximately $45,000-$60,000 annually, senior accountants $80,000-$120,000[^7]
- Big Four accounting firm partners: $500,000-$2,000,000 annually[^8]
- China: Junior accountants approximately 5,000-8,000 RMB monthly, CPAs 15,000-30,000 RMB[^9]

**Professional Threshold**:
- Requires passing professional exams (CPA, ACCA, CMA, etc.)
- Requires continuing education
- Requires understanding of constantly changing tax laws and accounting standards

## II. The Impact: What AI Did

The AI化 of the accounting industry is not a "future trend"—it is already happening.

**Automation of Basic Bookkeeping**:

AI can automatically complete the following tasks:[^10]
- Invoice processing and classification
- Bank reconciliation
- Expense reimbursement review
- Month-end closing
- Financial statement generation

Intuit (parent company of TurboTax and QuickBooks) launched an AI accounting assistant in 2023 that can automatically classify transactions, identify anomalies, and generate reports.[^11]

**Big Four Accounting Firm AI Deployments**:

| Company | AI Tool/Project | Impact |
|---------|-----------------|--------|
| Deloitte | Deloitte AI Institute | Basic audit work automation |
| PwC | GL.ai (AI audit tool) | Financial statement analysis automation |
| EY | EY.ai | Tax compliance automation |
| KPMG | KPMG Ignite | Risk assessment automation |

**AI Replacement Rate for Accounting Tasks**:

According to Deloitte's 2024 report:[^12]
- Basic bookkeeping: 90% automatable
- Invoice processing: 85% automatable
- Financial statement preparation: 70% automatable
- Tax filing (standard types): 80% automatable
- Audit sampling and testing: 60% automatable
- Tax planning (complex types): 20% automatable
- Management consulting: 15% automatable

**Chinese Market AI Accounting Tools**:

- **Yonyou YonGPT**: Enterprise-level AI financial assistant from Yonyou Network[^13]
- **Kingdee Cloud Star**: Kingdee International's AI financial management platform[^14]
- **PiaoYiTong**: AI invoice processing platform[^15]

## III. The Numbers: Layoffs/Income Decline/Industry Scale Changes

**Junior Accountant Demand Changes**:

According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics:[^16]
- 2022: The U.S. had 1.4 million accountants and auditors
- 2032 (projected): Decrease to 1.3 million, a reduction of 7.1%

This number may not seem large, but consider: before this, accounting industry employment had always been steadily growing. Declining demand means a fundamental shift in industry structure.

**Big Four Accounting Firm Changes**:

According to industry reports:[^17]
- 2023: The Big Four collectively cut approximately 5,000 positions
- 2024: Further cuts of approximately 3,000 positions
- Mainly affected: Junior auditors, basic tax personnel, bookkeepers

**SME Accounting Outsourcing Changes**:

AI accounting tools are changing SME accounting needs:[^18]
- Previously: Hired part-time accountants or outsourced to accounting firms
- Now: Using AI accounting tools to automatically handle basic accounting
- Result: Decreased demand for junior accounting personnel from SMEs

**Accounting Education Changes**:

According to AICPA (American Institute of Certified Public Accountants) report:[^19]
- 2023: Accounting undergraduate enrollment decreased by 7.8%
- Reason: Students worry AI will reduce accounting job opportunities
- Impact: Long-term, the accounting industry may face talent shortages

## V. Response: Who Adapted, Who Left

**Successfully Transitioned to AI Accounting Specialists**:

Some accountants successfully transitioned:
- Managing and reviewing AI accounting systems
- Providing professional judgment that AI systems cannot offer
- Focusing on complex tax planning and management consulting
- Becoming "financial data analysts"

**Fields Still Requiring Humans**:

- **Complex Tax Planning**: Requires understanding the nuances of tax law and specific business situations[^23]
- **Audit Judgment**: Requires professional judgment on anomalous transactions[^24]
- **Management Consulting**: Requires understanding business models and strategic direction[^25]
- **Interpersonal Relationships**: Building trust with clients and understanding their needs[^26]

**The Moat of Certification**:

An important characteristic of the accounting industry is **certification requirements**. Professional certifications like CPA, ACCA, and CMA are necessary conditions for practice.[^27] This provides accountants with some protection—AI can do basic work, but the签字 must be done by certified accountants.

But the value of this "moat" is being eroded. When AI does 80% of the work, certified accountants only need to review 20%. How much will the market pay for this 20%?

## Assessment

The AI化 of the accounting industry reveals a pattern: **the more standardized professional work is, the easier it is to replace with AI.**

This pattern is counterintuitive. We assume "professional" means "irreplaceable"—requiring exams, licenses, continuous learning. But AI doesn't care whether you have a license. It cares about: whether your work can be digitized, standardized, and automated.

The core of accounting work—bookkeeping, classification, reconciliation, report preparation—is highly standardized. Every transaction has a clear debit and credit, every number has a fixed format and position. This is precisely what AI excels at.

The Big Four's AI deployments reveal the industry's true intention: not to "augment" accountants but to "reduce" them. When AI can automatically complete 70% of audit work, the Big Four don't need "the same number of accountants doing higher-quality work"—they need "fewer accountants reviewing AI output."

Certification requirements are accountants' last line of defense. Certifications like CPA and ACCA are legal requirements that AI cannot replace. But the value of this defense line is being eroded. When AI does 80% of the work, the value of certified accountants shifts from "doing the work" to "signing off." The market won't price "signing off" the same as "doing the work."

The困境 of the accounting industry is a microcosm of a larger trend: **the depreciation of professional qualifications**. Law licenses, medical licenses, accounting licenses—these used to be guarantees of "iron rice bowls." But when AI can do most professional work, the value of licenses shifts from "skill certification" to "signing authority." Signing still requires humans, but not as many humans as before.

For accounting students, the reality is cruel: you need to learn what AI cannot do—complex tax planning, audit judgment, management consulting. But these "advanced" skills require years of experience accumulation, and the entry path—basic bookkeeping and auditing—is being closed by AI.

This is a paradox: you cannot skip the basics to reach the advanced, but the basics are disappearing.
