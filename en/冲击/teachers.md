# Teachers

> Teaching is considered one of the professions "hardest for AI to replace"—requiring emotional connection, personalized instruction, and role modeling. But AI is changing the形态 of education. When Khan Academy's Khanmigo can provide one-on-one math tutoring, when Duolingo uses AI to replace contract translators, when universities begin using AI to grade assignments—teachers face not "complete replacement" but "role redefinition."

## I. Before the Impact: What This Profession Used to Be

Teachers are the transmitters of human civilization. From ancient私塾 teachers to modern university professors, teachers have always been at the core of knowledge传承.

**Education Industry Size**:
- In 2023, global education expenditure was approximately $6 trillion[^1]
- U.S. education expenditure was approximately $1.4 trillion[^2]
- China's education expenditure was approximately 5 trillion RMB[^3]

**Number of Teachers**:
- Global: Approximately 70 million teachers[^4]
- United States: Approximately 3.7 million K-12 teachers[^5]
- China: Approximately 18 million teachers at all levels[^6]

**Teacher Income Levels**:
- United States: K-12 teachers approximately $60,000-$70,000 annually, university professors $80,000-$150,000[^7]
- China: Elementary school teachers approximately 5,000-8,000 RMB monthly, university professors 10,000-30,000 RMB[^8]

**What Teachers Do**:
- Teaching: Designing courses, lecturing, answering questions
- Assessment: Grading assignments, scoring exams, managing student grades
- Counseling: Communicating with students, providing psychological support
- Research: University teachers also need to conduct academic research

## II. The Impact: What AI Did

AI applications in education are expanding from "auxiliary tools" to "replacing部分 teaching functions."

**Personalized Learning**:

AI can provide personalized learning content and exercises based on each student's learning pace and style.[^9]

- **Khan Academy's Khanmigo**: AI tutoring assistant that can answer student questions one-on-one[^10]
- **Duolingo Max**: AI language learning assistant that can conduct conversation practice[^11]
- **Khan Academy**: AI-driven adaptive learning platform

**Automatic Grading**:

AI can automatically grade assignments and exams.[^12]
- Multiple choice and fill-in-the-blank: AI can grade 100% automatically
- Short answer questions: AI can grade most, with accuracy approximately 85-90%
- Essays: AI can provide preliminary assessment, but complex essays still require human grading

**AI Teaching Assistants**:

AI is becoming teachers' "assistants."[^13]
- Automatically generating lesson plans and courseware
- Automatically analyzing student learning data
- Automatically identifying students with learning difficulties
- Automatically recommending learning resources

Zhang Ming (pseudonym), head of a language training institution:[^14]

These tools can provide 24/7 speaking practice at only 1/10 the price of human teachers. We laid off 20 teachers, mainly those doing basic speaking practice."

**Case 3: AI-Assisted Teaching Success Story**

Wang Ming (pseudonym), elementary school teacher:[^16]

> "Our school started using an AI-assisted teaching system. AI can automatically analyze each student's learning progress, identify students with learning difficulties, and recommend personalized learning resources. This has improved my work efficiency significantly—data analysis that used to take hours now takes AI minutes. I can spend more time communicating with students."

## V. Response: Who Adapted, Who Left

**AI as a Teaching Tool**:

Successful education AI deployments are typically "augmentation" rather than "replacement":
- AI handles standardized teaching tasks (grading, practice, assessment)
- Teachers focus on non-standardized teaching tasks (inspiration, guidance, emotional support)
- AI provides data, teachers make decisions

**Fields Still Requiring Humans**:

- **Classroom Management**: Maintaining discipline, handling conflicts, creating learning atmosphere[^17]
- **Emotional Support**: Listening to students, providing psychological support, building trust[^18]
- **Creativity Cultivation**: Inspiring thinking, encouraging innovation, nurturing curiosity[^19]
- **Social Skills**: Teamwork, communication skills, empathy development[^20]

**Teachers Who Left the Industry**:

Many teachers chose to change careers:
- Transitioned to AI education tool development and support
- Transitioned to education management
- Transitioned to corporate training
- Completely left the education industry

## Assessment

The AI impact on the education industry has a unique dimension: **education is not just about transmitting knowledge, but about培养 people.**

AI can transmit knowledge—it can explain mathematical formulas, teach grammar rules, explain historical events. In these areas, AI may even be better than human teachers—because it can work 24/7, provide personalized instruction, and repeat explanations with infinite patience.

But education is not just about transmitting knowledge. Education is about培养 people—cultivating curiosity, creativity, critical thinking, empathy, and social skills. These abilities cannot be transmitted through AI. They require human-to-human interaction—requiring teachers' role modeling, cooperation between classmates, and classroom discussions and debates.

A teacher's value is not just "teaching knowledge." A good teacher can激发 students' curiosity, help them discover their interests and talents, and provide support and encouragement when they encounter difficulties. These are things AI cannot do.

But the market doesn't care about these things. The market cares about "efficiency"—transmitting the most knowledge at the lowest cost. AI wins decisively against human teachers on the "efficiency" dimension. When the market chooses AI over human teachers, education changes from "培养 people" to "transmitting knowledge."

Duolingo's case is a microcosm of this trend. Duolingo uses AI to replace contract translators and language teachers (see "Impact: Translation Industry"). Its slogan is "Learn languages with AI." But what Duolingo teaches is not "language"—it teaches "vocabulary and grammar." Language is culture, social interaction, and ways of thinking. Duolingo cannot teach you these things.

Khan Academy's Khanmigo is another case. It can answer math questions one-on-one and provide personalized learning paths. But it cannot teach you "why you should learn math"—it cannot let you感受 the beauty of mathematics, cannot help you understand how mathematics describes the world, cannot let you感受成就感 when solving difficult problems.

The future of the education industry may be: AI handles standardized knowledge transmission, while teachers focus on non-standardized human培养. But the实现 of this division of labor requires a前提: the education system is willing to pay for "human培养." If the education system is only willing to pay for "knowledge transmission," then teachers' value will be compressed to the minimum.

This is not the progress of education. This is the degradation of education—from "培养 people" degraded to "transmitting knowledge."
