# Human Resources

> Human Resources is a contradictory industry: it's called "Human" Resources, yet it's using AI to remove "humans" from its own work. Since 2023, AI resume screening, AI interviews, and automated recruitment processes are渗透 the HR industry at an alarming rate. HireVue's AI interview system has completed over 30 million interviews cumulatively[^1]; Eightfold AI claims to reduce hiring cycles by 50%[^2]. HR specialists—those responsible for screening resumes, scheduling interviews, and processing onboarding—are becoming a high-risk group for AI replacement. When an industry's core function is "managing people," and this very function is being接管 by algorithms, it faces not just an efficiency revolution but an existential crisis.

## I. Before the Impact: What This Profession Used to Be

Human Resources (HR) is the "central nervous system" of a company—responsible for the entire employee lifecycle management from recruitment to departure.

**Professional Categories**:

- **Recruiter/Recruitment Manager**: Posting positions, screening resumes, organizing interviews, extending offers
- **HR Specialist/HR Generalist**: Handling daily personnel affairs—attendance, compensation, social insurance, onboarding/offboarding procedures
- **HRBP (HR Business Partner)**: Liaising with business departments, providing human resource solutions
- **Learning & Development Specialist**: Designing and implementing employee training programs
- **Compensation & Benefits Specialist**: Designing and managing compensation systems
- **Employee Relations Specialist**: Handling labor disputes and employee complaints

**Market Size**:

- In 2023, the global human resource services market was valued at approximately $650 billion[^3]
- Of which the HR technology market was approximately $35 billion[^4]
- China's HR services market was approximately 1.2 trillion RMB[^5]

**Number of Practitioners**:

- United States: Approximately 780,000 HR specialists and managers[^6]
- China: Approximately 8 million HR practitioners[^7]
- Global: Approximately 30 million HR practitioners[^8]

**Income Levels**:

- United States: HR specialists approximately $50,000-$60,000 annually, HR managers approximately $80,000-$120,000, HRD approximately $150,000-$250,000[^9]
- China: HR specialists approximately 5,000-8,000 RMB monthly, HR managers 10,000-20,000 RMB, HRD 30,000-50,000 RMB[^10]

**Professional Threshold**:

- HR is one of the few professional occupations with "no hard准入 threshold"
- United States: SHRM-CP/SHRM-SCP certifications are加分项, but not必需[^11]
- China: Enterprise HR Manager professional qualification certificate (adjusted to socialized level certification in 2021)[^12]
- Most HR positions only require a bachelor's degree and relevant experience

**Work Model**:

Junior HR daily work is highly repetitive and process-driven:
- **Recruitment**: Post positions → Screen resumes → Phone screening → Schedule interviews → Follow up → Extend offer → Process onboarding
- **Employee Relations**: Attendance management → Social insurance and housing fund → Transfers and separations → Labor contract management
- **Training**: Needs assessment → Course scheduling → Training implementation → Effectiveness evaluation
- **Compensation**: Payroll calculation → Tax filing → Performance statistics

A large portion of this work is "process-driven"—following fixed rules, processing standardized information, making predictable decisions.

## II. The Impact: What AI Did

HR is one of the earliest industries to embrace AI—because a large portion of HR work恰好 is what AI excels at: processing massive amounts of standardized information.

**AI Resume Screening**:

- **LinkedIn Recruiter**: AI can automatically match and rank candidates based on job requirements[^13]
- **Eightfold AI**: Uses deep learning to analyze candidate resumes and provide match scores. Claims accuracy exceeds 90%[^14]
- **HireVue**: AI video interview system that analyzes candidates' language content, tone, and facial expressions to assess "competency"[^15]
- **Moka** (China): AI-driven recruitment management system with automatic resume parsing and intelligent recommendations[^16]

AI resume screening is over 1,000 times faster than humans. A human HR can screen 50-100 resumes per day; AI can process 10,000 in seconds.[^17]

**AI Interviews**:

AI interviews are the most controversial area of HR AI化:
- **HireVue**: Has cumulatively completed over 30 million AI video interviews[^18]. AI analyzes candidates' response content, tone, facial expressions, and micro-expressions to generate assessment reports.
- **MyInterview**: AI asynchronous video interview platform[^19]
- **Spark Hire**: AI-assisted video interview tool[^20]

Controversies surrounding AI interviews:
- **Bias Issues**: HireVue's AI interview system has been accused of racial and gender bias[^21]. In 2021, HireVue was forced to abandon facial expression analysis features[^22]
- **Transparency**: Candidates don't know how AI evaluates them; evaluation criteria are not公开[^23]
- **Fairness**: AI may favor candidates with特定 accents, expressions, or behavioral patterns

**Automated Recruitment Processes**:

- **Intelligent Job Posting**: AI automatically optimizes job descriptions based on position requirements and selects最佳 posting channels
- **Automatic Interview Scheduling**: AI automatically coordinates candidate and interviewer schedules without manual coordination
- **Automatic Background Checks**: AI automatically verifies candidates' education and work experience
- **Automatic Offer Generation**: Automatically generates compensation packages based on candidate background and market data

**Employee Management AI化**:

Beyond recruitment, AI is also渗透 other HR areas:
- **Intelligent Scheduling**: AI automatically schedules based on employee skills, preferences, and business needs[^24]
- **Employee Sentiment Monitoring**: Monitoring employee sentiment and turnover risk by analyzing internal communication data (emails, Slack messages)[^25]
- **Training Personalization**: AI automatically recommends training content based on employee能力差距[^26]
- **Compensation Benchmarking**: AI automatically analyzes market compensation data and provides real-time compensation recommendations[^27]

**Chinese Market HR AI Tools**:

- **Moka**: AI recruitment management system, serving over 2,000 enterprises[^28]
- **Beisen**: Integrated HR SaaS platform with AI capabilities[^29]
- **GaiaWorks**: AI intelligent scheduling and workforce management platform[^30]
- **Xinrenxinshi**: AI-driven human resource management platform[^31]

## III. The Numbers: Layoffs/Income Decline/Industry Scale Changes

**HR Position Changes**:

According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics:[^32]
- 2022: The U.S. had 782,600 HR specialists
- 2032 (projected): Grow to 837,400, growth of 7%
- But growth is concentrated in senior HR roles (HRBP, HRD); junior HR position growth has明显 slowed

**HR Tech Funding and Layoffs Coexist**:

2023-2024, the HR tech industry presents a contradictory态势:
- HR Tech venture capital investment totaled approximately $12 billion (2023)[^33]
- But multiple HR tech companies are also laying off: Workday laid off approximately 1,500 people (2024)[^34]; BambooHR laid off approximately 300 people
- Reasons for layoffs: slowing growth + AI improving HR tech companies' own efficiency

**Corporate HR Department Downsizing**:

According to SHRM (Society for Human Resource Management) survey:[^35]
- In 2023, approximately 35% of U.S. companies reported introducing AI tools in HR departments
- In these companies, HR department headcount decreased by an average of 15%-20%
- Main affected positions: recruitment coordinators, HR specialists, training assistants

**Changes in China's HR Industry**:

- Since 2023, internet industry HR positions have decreased by approximately 25% (unverified)[^36]
- Headhunting industry impacted: AI resume matching reduced demand for junior headhunters[^37]
- Traditional HR outsourcing companies face transformation pressure

**Changes in HR Consulting and Outsourcing**:

AI has changed the business model of HR outsourcing:
- **Previously**: Companies outsourced HR functions like recruitment and payroll to专业 companies, which relied on manpower to deliver
- **Now**: AI tools allow companies to complete most basic HR work themselves, reducing outsourcing demand
- **Result**: Small and medium HR outsourcing companies face生存危机[^38]

**Changes in HR Certification**:

HR certification bodies like SHRM are adjusting exam content:
- Starting 2024, SHRM exams added AI and data analysis modules[^39]
- HR certification is shifting from "professional skill certification" to "AI literacy certification"
- The industry is开始讨论: do HR practitioners need to learn programming and data science?

## V. Response: Who Adapted, Who Left

**Those Who Adapted**:

- **Strategic HRBP**: Shifting from "process execution" to "business strategic partner," using AI data to support高层 decisions
- **Employee Experience Designer**: Focusing on areas AI cannot replace—corporate culture building, employee care, conflict mediation
- **HR Data Analyst**: Using AI tools to analyze talent data and provide talent strategy recommendations
- **AI-HR Product Manager**: Designing AI products at HR tech companies, requiring HR expertise + technical understanding

**Those Who Left**:

- **Recruitment Coordinators**: AI automated resume screening, interview scheduling, and communication follow-up
- **Payroll Specialists**: AI can automatically complete payroll calculations and social insurance filings
- **Training Administrators**: AI automatically schedules training, recommends courses, and evaluates effectiveness
- **Career Transition Directions**: Project management, administrative management, operations, trainers (focused on offline delivery)

**Fields Still Requiring Humans**:

- **Executive Recruitment**: C-level recruitment requires deep relationship maintenance and industry insights that AI cannot replace[^46]
- **Labor Relations and Dispute Resolution**: Requires face-to-face communication, emotional安抚, and legal judgment
- **Corporate Culture Building**: Requires warm,持续 human interaction[^47]
- **Layoffs and Organizational Change Management**: Laid-off employees need a "person" to tell them this news, not an algorithm
- **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI)**: Requires deep understanding of different groups' experiences and needs[^48]

**HR Industry Reflection**:

AI impact is forcing the HR industry to reflect on its own value positioning:
- If HR's core work is "managing processes," then AI确实 can replace it
- If HR's core value is "understanding people"—recruiting the most suitable people, retaining the best people, helping the most struggling people—then AI is far from being able to replace it
- The problem is: in most companies' HR departments, the former accounts for a larger proportion than the latter

## Assessment

The AI impact story of Human Resources is a story of **the separation of "management" and "care."**

The name "Human Resources" itself is contradictory—it calls "humans" "resources." In the industrial age, this made sense: workers were factors of production, and HR was responsible for managing this factor of production—recruitment, scheduling, payroll, processing departures. This logic has运行 for a hundred years.

AI is exposing the ultimate consequence of this logic: **if you treat people as resources to manage, then resource management can be automated.** Resumes are data, attendance is data, payroll is data, performance is data—AI's ability to process data surpasses any human. When HR's core work is "managing data," AI replacing HR is only a matter of time.

But "people" are not "resources"—this is the lesson the HR industry is being forced to relearn.

When an employee cries in a conference room—because of family difficulties, work stress, or即将 being laid off—they don't need an EAP hotline number pushed by an AI system, but a real, warm person sitting across from them, offering a tissue. When a team faces reorganization—people anxious, trust collapsing—what's needed is not an AI-generated "communication strategy document," but a trustworthy HR presence, listening, coordinating.

This "human touch" is恰恰 what the HR industry has always忽略—because under efficiency logic, it's "inefficient." Arranging for an HR to spend two hours talking with a difficult employee不如 having an AI system automatically push a mental health app. But from a "human" perspective, the value of these two is天壤之别.

The AI impact on the HR industry reveals a larger trend: **any profession that treats "people" as "processes" to manage will be replaced by AI.** Not just HR—customer service, administration,部分 management functions—as long as your core value is "managing processes" rather than "understanding people," AI will do it better than you.

But this also意味着 a reverse opportunity: **if HR can transform from "process manager" to "people understander"—truly understanding employees' needs, fears, motivations, and potential—it will become more valuable than ever.** Because in an algorithm-dominated work environment, people need a "person" to represent their interests more than ever.

The question is: is HR ready? Most HR is still busy learning AI tools—rather than思考 a more fundamental question: when AI can do all "management" work, what is the purpose of HR's existence?

The answer may be: **HR should not be the department that manages people—it should be the department that守护 people.** Defending human dignity, needs, and emotions in the face of algorithms. This is the truly irreplaceable value of HR in the AI era.

But this requires the HR industry to complete an identity revolution—from "resource management" to "humanity guardianship." This is harder than learning any AI tool.
