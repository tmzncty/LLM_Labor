# Venezuelan Data Workers: Dollar-Paid Gig Work in an Unstable Economy

> **v2 audit: 2026-08-28.** Venezuela should not be reduced to a stereotype of universally impoverished annotators. What can be documented is that some educated and older workers use platforms such as Appen, Remotasks and Clickworker to earn foreign-currency income from AI training and other digital gigs, while accepting volatility and weak labor protection.

Rest of World profiled several Venezuelan online workers in 2024. A former legal professional identified as I.I. used Remotasks and Appen for AI training and annotation. A computer-science professor also participated in digital platform work, while 53-year-old graphic designer and video editor Omar Alfonzo earned about $400 per month across online gigs.[^1]

These cases complicate the “cheap low-skill labor” story. Some participants bring legal, technical and creative professional backgrounds. Economic instability pushes them into a global digital labor market where those skills are repackaged into task-based services.

## Why platform work attracts workers

Cross-border platforms can offer access to foreign demand, dollar-linked payments and work outside local employers. But workers absorb the costs of hardware, connectivity, outages, training, qualification tests and empty task queues.

The result is a form of platform dependence: workers choose when to log in, but they do not control project supply, account policy or regional availability.

Remotasks' abrupt restrictions in other countries in 2024 illustrate this platform risk, but that event should not be falsely described as a blanket shutdown of Venezuelan workers.[^2]

## Evidence boundaries

- Interview cases do not establish average Venezuelan annotator earnings.
- Omar's ~$400/month covered online gig work broadly, not a standard AI-labeling wage.
- Participants are not uniformly poor or low-skilled.
- Other countries' platform bans should not be projected onto Venezuela.

The historical importance of the case is the tradeoff itself: access to a global market can create real opportunity while transferring employment security from institutions to individual platform accounts.
