## 3. Impact Level Criteria

| Level | Definition | Reference Indicators |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| **High** | 50%+ positions significantly reduced or transformed within 3 years | Cost reduction >80%, automation rate >60%, hiring reduction >40% |
| **Medium** | 20-50% positions affected, primarily entry-level / repetitive work | Cost reduction 50-80%, automation rate 30-60%, efficiency gain >50% |
| **Low** | <20% positions affected, primarily tool-assisted augmentation | Efficiency gain >30%, but total position count essentially stable |

## 4. Data Source Notes

- **Industry Reports**: Slator, Gartner, Deloitte, Thomson Reuters, and other consulting firm annual reports
- **Company Data**: Financial reports, product launches, official blogs
- **Industry Hearsay**: Practitioner communities (LinkedIn, Blind, 1point3acres), recruitment platform data, unpublished interviews
- **Academic Research**: Anthropic RLAIF paper, GitHub Copilot efficiency study

> Note: All data points marked "industry hearsay" require further verification. Annotator counts are estimates and fluctuate significantly (under the crowdsourcing model).
