# Impacted Occupations: Evidence Status, Not Replacement Scores

This table no longer assigns a fake “AI replacement probability.” Occupations contain many tasks and can experience automation, augmentation, entry-level compression and overall employment growth at the same time.

| Field | Observed AI effect | Audit judgment | Key caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Translation | Drafting and post-editing automate routine text | Strong task substitution; occupation persists | Language/domain heterogeneity |
| Illustration | Cheap synthetic supply and commission pressure | Bargaining pressure on creative labor | Voluntary surveys are not universal rates |
| Customer service | Routine tier-1 conversations automated | Clear routine-task substitution | FTE-equivalent workload ≠ layoffs |
| Junior programming | Boilerplate, testing and documentation automated | Entry tasks compressed; higher-level judgment remains | Tech cycle confounds causality |
| Journalism | Summaries, rewrites, template text | Workflow change, not sole cause of newsroom cuts | Advertising/business decline matters |
| Accounting | Reconciliation, entry and review tools | Occupational stratification | Accountants and clerks have different outlooks |
| Legal assistants | Search, summary and document review | Lower-level document work exposed | Responsibility and practice rules constrain replacement |
| Music | Synthetic supply grows | Individual pay pressure can coexist with aggregate royalty growth | Aggregate revenue ≠ individual income |
| Medical diagnostics | Screening and decision support | Assistance/review, not “AI broadly beats doctors” | Benchmarks ≠ clinical workflow |
| 3D art | Concept, texture and asset assistance | Workflow restructuring | Game-industry cycle is a major confounder |
| Teaching | Planning, feedback and tutoring assistance | Augmentation evidence stronger than replacement | Education context varies |
| Financial analysis | Extraction, summaries, draft models | Task automation with continued professional demand | Regulation and accountability matter |
| Architecture | Rendering and option generation | Design workflow change | Licensed responsibility remains human-centered |
| Counseling | Chat systems and documentation | Adjunct use plus safety risk | Conversation quality ≠ clinical efficacy |
| HR | Screening and ranking | Recruitment governance/algorithmic management central | Bias, privacy and recourse matter |
| Entry-level jobs | Routine cognitive entry tasks exposed | Monitor closely | Macro hiring cycle confounds effects |
| India IT services | Coding, testing and support automation | Service model restructuring | Firm strategy and global IT demand co-determine outcomes |
