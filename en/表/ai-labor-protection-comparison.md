# AI and Labor Governance Comparison — Not a Country Ranking

Updated 2026-08-28. Different jurisdictions use binding AI law, existing discrimination/data law, soft-law guidance and collective bargaining. A single “protection score” would be misleading.

| Jurisdiction/mechanism | Layer | Labor relevance | 2026 status |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU AI Act | Binding regulation | Certain recruitment and worker-management uses are Annex III high-risk; workplace deployers face notice/human-oversight duties | Employment high-risk rules apply **2 Dec 2027** after the AI Omnibus; Article 50 transparency rules apply from 2 Aug 2026 |
| EU Quality Jobs Act | Consultation/proposal | Algorithmic management, AI at work, excessive monitoring, subcontracting chains | Second-stage social-partner consultation launched July 2026; not yet an enacted Act |
| US federal | Existing civil-rights law | EEOC says federal anti-discrimination law applies when AI is used in hiring and employment decisions | No single comprehensive federal workplace-AI statute identified in this audit |
| US DOL guidance | Nonbinding guidance | Human oversight, transparency, worker voice, data protection, training | 2024 material; DOL pages warn pre-Jan-2025 releases may not reflect current policy |
| US sector bargaining | Binding contracts for covered parties | WGA/SAG-AFTRA rules on training, replicas, consent, notice and compensation | Expanded in 2025–2026 agreements |
| UK | Equality Act + data/ADM law + regulator guidance | Non-discrimination, transparency, bias safeguards, challenge/human review in automated recruitment | ICO active oversight in 2026 under updated Data (Use and Access) Act framework |
| Japan | Existing law + soft-law business guidance | Human-centric rights, safety, transparency and governance; civil-liability interpretation | AI Guidelines for Business v1.2 in 2026; no comprehensive national workplace-AI statute identified in this audit |

This table is not legal advice and does not cover US state/local rules or every sectoral regime.
