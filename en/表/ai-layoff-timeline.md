## Why there is no “total AI layoffs” number

Adding these rows would combine incompatible measures: completed layoffs, future plans, workload equivalents, hiring decisions, and simultaneous hiring into different roles.

A credible archive should therefore record event type, company attribution, alternative causes, and later execution rather than manufacturing one dramatic total.
