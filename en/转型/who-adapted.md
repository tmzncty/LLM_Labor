# Who Adapted: Task Recomposition, Not Hero Stories

The strongest evidence on adaptation comes from large samples, not anecdotal career-success profiles.

Danish administrative data show little average change in earnings or hours two years after ChatGPT, but widespread new tasks in AI content generation, oversight and integration.[^1]

A randomized field experiment across 66 firms and 7,137 knowledge workers found that treated users spent roughly two fewer hours per week on email and worked less outside normal hours, without a detectable broad restructuring of task composition.[^2]

US workforce-training data covering 1.9 million training spells suggest that returns to retraining have risen for workers from highly AI-exposed occupations.[^3]

Early adaptation therefore looks like task recomposition, selective time savings, occupational switching and formal retraining—not a universal “learn AI and win” story.
