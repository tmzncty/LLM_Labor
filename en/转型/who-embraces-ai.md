# Who Is Embracing AI: Adoption Is Mostly Augmentation So Far

Using the 2026 US Census BTOS AI supplement, NBER researchers find that 18% of firms used AI in at least one function during Nov 2025–Jan 2026 (32% employment-weighted). Adoption is concentrated in large, knowledge-intensive firms but usually remains narrow.[^1]

Among adopters, 66% report using AI for task augmentation; only about 2% report employment reductions. Worker use can also occur without a formal firm-wide adoption program.[^1]

Earlier national survey evidence found 23% of employed US respondents had used generative AI for work in the previous week by late 2024.[^2]

Meta's failed 2026 Project OT is an important counterexample: aggressive AI-native reorganization can stall because of worker resistance, poor reliability, security problems and disappointing productivity.[^3]

“Embracing AI” should therefore be documented as organizational experimentation, not as a synonym for successful automation.
